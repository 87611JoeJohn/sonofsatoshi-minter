📖 Guide⚙ Settings

Your collections

1Collection and art

Name it, then add every image. They're numbered 0001, 0002 … in the order you pick them (sorted by file name).

2Rarity and stories

✨ Your local AI looks at every piece, gives it traits and a rarity tier, and writes its story from what's actually in the picture. Around 20–60 seconds per piece; you can close the page and come back.

Canon: facts the stories must respect (optional)
AI is off. Turn it on in Settings if you have Ollama, or use the manual option below.
Manual: my own description and trait weights (no AI)

Optional traits: each category picks a value per piece by weight (higher = more common), then pieces are scored and tiered.

3Review and edit

See each piece the way wallets will show it. Change any name, story, trait or rarity before you mint.

4Store it on IPFS

Uploads the art and stories to your storage and writes the final contract. Run this again any time you edit something (then use Re-point on the mint page if you've already minted).

5Deploy and mint

Opens the wallet page: connect Xverse or Leather, deploy your contract, mint (in batches of 200 for big collections). Everything is approved in your wallet.
