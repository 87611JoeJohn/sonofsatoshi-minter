1Connect your wallet

Phantom, Solflare or Backpack (browser extension). The wallet pays and approves every step; this app never sees a key.

2Create the collection

One account on Solana holds the collection's name, its picture and the royalty rule for every piece in it.

3Mint the pieces

Your wallet approves a whole batch at once (a few pieces per transaction). Big collections take several batches; if anything stops halfway, come back and press the button again: pieces already minted are skipped.

Connect a wallet to see the cost.

4Manage

Re-point after you stored an edited version (step 4 on the main page): every minted piece is switched to the new links. Royalty can be changed any time. Lock freezes the names and links of the collection and every piece in it, forever.

🔒 Lock forever

After locking, nobody (you included) can change a name, a story or a picture link again. Do it only when everything is final. Type LOCK to enable the button.
