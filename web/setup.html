📖 Guide

Welcome 👋

This app turns a folder of your art into a finished NFT collection on Stacks (which settles on Bitcoin), directly on Bitcoin as Ordinals, on Solana, or on Ethereum and Base. It numbers your pieces, gives each one a rarity and a story, stores everything on IPFS, and lets you deploy and mint from your own wallet.

Your keys stay yours. This app runs only on your computer. It never asks for your seed phrase or private key. Every transaction is approved by you, inside your wallet.

Setup takes about 5 minutes. You'll need:

  • A wallet (browser extension): Xverse or Leather for Stacks and Ordinals; Phantom, Solflare or Backpack for Solana; MetaMask, Rabby or any browser wallet for Ethereum and Base. A Ledger works through them.
  • A place to store the art (next step explains): your own IPFS node, or a free Pinata account.
  • Optional: Ollama if you want the AI to write the stories.

Not sure about something? The Guide explains every part in plain words.

What are you minting on?

🟣 StacksA Bitcoin layer with smart contracts: royalties, re-pointing, freezing. The art lives on IPFS.
🟠 Bitcoin OrdinalsInscribed directly on Bitcoin itself: the art lives on-chain forever. No IPFS, no contract, no royalties.
💠 Ethereum / BaseYour own ERC-721 contract: royalties, re-pointing, freezing. Base is Ethereum's low-fee layer (cents instead of dollars). MetaMask, Rabby or any browser wallet. The art lives on IPFS.
🟪 SolanaMetaplex Core collections: cheap and fast, royalties, re-pointing, locking. The art lives on IPFS. Phantom, Solflare or Backpack.

Your wallet address

Your finished NFTs are minted to this address, and royalties are paid to it. It's a public address: safe to share, like an account number.

Where do I find it?

Xverse: tap Receive → copy the Stacks address (not the Bitcoin one).
Leather: click Receive → Stacks → copy.
Testnet addresses start with ST, mainnet with SP. Using a Ledger? Pick the Ledger account in your wallet first.

Never type your 12/24 secret words anywhere in this app, or on any website. Nobody legitimate will ever ask.

Where your art lives (IPFS)

An NFT on the blockchain is a pointer; the picture and its story live on IPFS. If nobody keeps your files online, your NFTs show up as blank boxes. Read why →

How NFTs link to the art (advanced)

Solana wallets need https links. They go through your public gateway if you have one, otherwise through ipfs.io.

AI stories and rarity (optional)

With Ollama (free, runs on your own computer), the app looks at every picture, gives it traits and a rarity tier, and writes its backstory. You review and edit everything before minting. Without it, you set traits yourself.

Royalty and finish

A royalty is the share of each resale that marketplaces pay back to you (for those that honor it). You can change it per collection, and even after minting.
