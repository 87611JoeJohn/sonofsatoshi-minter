1Prepare the pieces

On Bitcoin you pay for every byte, so size is price. A well-compressed WebP of a few dozen KB costs a small fraction of a big PNG and looks the same on a phone. Nothing is inscribed until step 2.

Compress all pieces to WebP

2Pay and inscribe

The app makes a batch with its own temporary key (saved on this computer so the money can always be recovered), and gives you ONE address and an exact amount. Pay it from any Bitcoin wallet. The inscriptions go to your ordinals address: …

Up to 50 pieces per batch; big collections go in several batches.
