1Connect your wallet

Xverse or Leather. Using a Ledger? Pick the Ledger account in the wallet first. The wallet that deploys becomes the owner of the collection, the only one who can mint or change it later.

2Deploy the contract

Puts your collection's contract on the blockchain (a one-time fee, usually well under 1 STX). You approve it in your wallet.

See the contract code

    
    

  


  

    
3Mint

    
Creates the NFTs and sends them to your address. Up to 200 per approval; big collections
      take a few rounds. Each round waits for the last to confirm.

    

    

    
    

  


  

    
4After minting (any time later)

    
Re-point to your latest edits

    
Edited stories or art and pressed Store on IPFS again? This points the contract at the new version.

    

    

    
Ask wallets to refresh

    
Tells Hiro, which Xverse, Leather and marketplaces read from, to re-read every piece. Use it after a re-point, or if pictures show as blank boxes.

Freeze forever (optional, can't be undone)

    
Locks the art and stories permanently. Nobody can change them again, including you. Collectors like this
      because it proves the art won't change. Only do it once everything is final.
