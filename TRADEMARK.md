# The SonOfSatoshi name and logo

The **code** is free under the [AGPL-3.0](LICENSE): use it, study it, change it, share it.

The **name "SonOfSatoshi"** and the **logo** are not part of that license. They identify the original project and its
creator. You're welcome to say your project is "based on SonOfSatoshi Minter". But if you publish a changed version,
please give it **your own name and logo**, so people aren't confused about who made what, and so nobody gets blamed
for bugs, or a scam, in a copy they didn't make.
